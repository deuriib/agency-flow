# FRAME — example-marketing-campaign

## Goal
Launch a targeted product-led growth email and landing page campaign highlighting time-to-value to increase sign-up conversion rate by 15% over 30 days.

## Success criteria (measurable)
- [ ] Landing page visitor-to-signup conversion rate increases by at least 15% compared to baseline.
- [ ] Email campaign open rate exceeds 35% and click-through rate exceeds 8%.
- [ ] Brand and legal review sign-off secured for all copy assets.

## Scope
- In: Landing page redesign copy, 3-part email drip sequence, UTM tracking setup.
- Out: Paid social ad spend, video production.

## Domain profile
- Domains involved: Marketing, Content, Design, Growth
- Deliverable type: campaign
- Optional phases: research? yes — measure? yes

## Constraints
- Must launch prior to Q4 product update announcement.

## Assigned team
- Domain: Product Marketer, Content Writer, UI Designer — campaign strategy, copy, and design.
- Process steward: Studio Producer — resource allocation and schedule feasibility.

## Gates
- Domain gate: Product Marketer — PASS
- Process gate: Studio Producer — PASS
