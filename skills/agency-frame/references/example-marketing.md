# FRAME — q2-developer-outreach (worked example)

## Goal

Drive 500 qualified signups from developers in our ICP during Q2 via a
multi-channel campaign built on the RESEARCH findings, without paid-social
spend.

## Success criteria (measurable)

- [ ] ≥ 500 ICP signups attributed to the campaign by end of Q2.
- [ ] Cost per signup ≤ $0 (organic-only; newsletter + talks + README).
- [ ] ≥ 60% of signups activate the product within 7 days.
- [ ] ≥ 2 published conference talks referencing the campaign message.

## Scope

- In: campaign narrative, landing page, 3 short demos, newsletter series,
  2 conference talk submissions, README updates on 3 OSS repos.
- Out: paid social, influencer deals, pricing changes.

## Domain profile

- Domains involved: Marketing, Content, Design, Product
- Deliverable type: campaign
- Optional phases: research? yes — measure? yes

## Constraints

- Zero paid media spend.
- All claims must be backed by the RESEARCH evidence.
- Launch in week 4 of Q2; talks submitted by week 2.

## Assigned team

- Domain: Growth Hacker (Marketing) — owns the campaign goal and success
  criteria.
- Domain: Sprint Prioritizer (Product) — validates that every criterion
  is measurable from available analytics.
- Domain: Studio Producer — declares the four divisions are staffed and
  available across the Q2 window.
- Process steward: Senior Project Manager — confirms scope is achievable
  within Q2 without external dependencies.

## Gates

- Domain gate: Growth Hacker — PASS
- Process gate: Senior Project Manager — PASS (staffing confirmed by
  Studio Producer; all criteria measurable; no unowned constraint)
