# MEASURE — q2-developer-outreach (worked example)

## Metrics collected

- Signups attributed: 512 (target 500 — exceeded).
- Cost per signup: $0 (target ≤ $0 — met).
- 7-day activation: 64% (target ≥ 60% — met).
- Talks confirmed: 2 (target ≥ 2 — met).
- Newsletter: 5 sends, 42% open, 6.1% click, 0.9% unsubscribe.
- Landing: 3.1% visitor → signup conversion; mobile CTA fix raised it to 3.8%.
- Demos: 78% completion on shortest (2.0 min), 71% on 2.5 min, 61% on 2.8 min.
- Blog: 4,200 reads, 11% click-through to signup.
- Attribution channel mix: talks 38%, newsletter 34%, blog 18%, README 10%.

## Per-criterion outcome vs FRAME

- Criterion 1 — 512 / 500 → exceeded.
- Criterion 2 — $0 / $0 → met.
- Criterion 3 — 64% / 60% → exceeded.
- Criterion 4 — 2 / 2 → met.

## Learnings

- Newsletter #3 outperformed by 2.1× — its subject line was a specific
  number ("40% p95 reduction"); the others were generic.
- Conference talks drove 38% of signups despite being the smallest channel
  by volume — depth beats breadth for this audience.
- Landing page mobile CTA placement was the single biggest conversion
  lever (+0.7pp on visitor → signup).
- Demo length inversely correlated with completion (78% → 61% from 2.0 min
  to 2.8 min), consistent with the RESEARCH finding.
