# RESEARCH — anti-patterns

## 1. Research without questions

Opening the phase file and listing sources first, then inventing questions
to justify them. Fix: write questions before touching any source.

## 2. Sources that aren't sources

"Best practices", "common knowledge", "the team knows". Fix: replace with
a URL, file path, or interview ID — or drop the finding.

## 3. Findings with no forward link

A finding that appears in `## Findings` but not in `## Findings → FRAME
criteria`. Fix: either map it, or move it to an appendix labelled
"informational".

## 4. The "let's do research" trap

Running RESEARCH because it feels rigorous, without a FRAME-declared
`research? yes`. Fix: refuse to open the phase. Research without a
decision is expensive theater.

## 5. Retroactive research

Writing findings after FRAME criteria are chosen, to fit the criteria.
Fix: if the criteria came first, RESEARCH was optional and should be
declared `no` — not retrofitted.

## 6. Question inflation

Nine research questions for a project that will fit in five phase files.
Fix: cap at 5. More questions = unclear goal.
