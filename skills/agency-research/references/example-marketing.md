# RESEARCH — q2-developer-outreach (worked example)

## Research questions

1. Which channels do developers in our ICP use to discover infra tools?
2. Which messages convert best in past campaigns (tone, proof type)?
3. What content formats have the highest completion rate for our audience?

## Sources cited

- Past campaign analytics: `analytics/campaigns/2025-Q4.json`.
- 6 customer interviews (transcripts in `research/interviews/`).
- Developer survey (n=412), 2025-Q4 industry report.

## Findings

- Q1: Newsletter + GitHub README mention + targeted conference talks
  outperform paid social by 3× on signup rate.
- Q2: Concrete latency/cost numbers beat abstract "scalability" claims.
  Proof-by-example outperforms testimonials for this audience.
- Q3: Short technical demos (< 3 min) have 78% completion vs 22% for
  long-form video. Blog posts with code samples have 61% read-through.

## Findings → FRAME criteria (preview)

- Q1 supports FRAME criterion 1 (500 signups via newsletter + talks +
  README) and criterion 2 ($0 CPS — no paid social).
- Q2 supports FRAME criterion 4 (talks referencing concrete numbers).
- Q3 informs PROPOSE deliverable choices (short demos, blog with code
  samples), not a FRAME criterion directly.
